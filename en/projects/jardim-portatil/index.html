Jardim Portátil NF.3.0

Espaço Cubo, Galeria Fernando Santos, Porto, 21.03–30.05.2026

Jardim Portátil NF.3.0 (Portable Garden NF.3.0) occupied the Espaço Cubo at Galeria Fernando Santos, Porto, from 21 March to 30 May 2026. Three wall sculptures from the Naturfatura 04 series were shown alongside the installation.

Tamed Nature

CAM, Fundação Calouste Gulbenkian, Lisbon, 2002

This project investigates the way we relate to nature in urban environments. Tamed Nature is a group of constructions that play with the idea of nature as a consumable item. These constructions of artificial nature combine the city dweller's desire to be close to the wilderness and his need for physical comfort.

Fabric, carpet, acrylic paint, styrofoam, wood, variable dimensions.

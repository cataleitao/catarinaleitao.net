Drawing Box

Quase Galeria, Porto, 2017 · Drawing Room, Madrid, 2018 · Fundação Eugénio de Almeida, Évora, 2019

Drawing Box, 2018. Wood, MDF, watercolor paper, acrylic ink, cotton thread, variable dimensions (approx. 250x300x200 cm).

The first Drawing Box was shown in 2017 at Quase Galeria, Porto, in an exhibition curated by Fátima Lambert.

Biography

Catarina Leitão’s artistic practice explores the relationships between nature and culture through processes of construction, transformation, and adaptation. Working across sculpture, installation, drawing, and the artist’s book, she invents objects, landscapes, and devices that bring together references to the natural world and human-made artefacts. Experimentation and material manipulation are central to her practice, where making and thinking inform one another.

Her work has been exhibited in Portugal and internationally, notably in New York, where she lived for fifteen years. It has been shown at institutions including MoMA PS1, Socrates Sculpture Park, and the Bronx Museum of the Arts. She has held solo exhibitions at the Centro de Arte Moderna, Fundação Calouste Gulbenkian, Sintra Museum of Modern Art – Berardo Collection, Casa da Cerca – Centro de Arte Contemporânea, Museu Nacional de Soares dos Reis, and Centro Cultural de Cascais. Her work is held in collections including the Portuguese State Contemporary Art Collection, Fundação Calouste Gulbenkian, Fundação EDP, and Fundação PLMJ.

She received a joint scholarship from the Fundação Calouste Gulbenkian and the Luso-American Development Foundation (FLAD, 1997–99), as well as grants and fellowships from the Pollock-Krasner Foundation (2001–02) and the New York Foundation for the Arts (2009). She has participated in residency programmes at the Sharpe Foundation (2004–05), Center for Book Arts (2007), and Museu do Caramulo (2025–26).

She holds a PhD in Contemporary Art from the University of Coimbra (2022), an MFA from Hunter College, New York (2000), and a degree in Painting from the University of Lisbon (1993). She taught at ESAD.CR from 2011 to 2024 and currently teaches at the University of Coimbra’s Colégio das Artes and, during the first semester of 2026–27, at the School of Architecture, Art and Design of the University of Minho.
